Using MedEmbed as base, tested 9 configurations:

E. RRF + Reranker Combination

FINAL SELECTED CONFIGURATION
-----------------------------
MedEmbed + BM25 RRF [0.7, 0.3] (k=10)
- Precision@5: 0.427
- Latency: 369ms
- Weights: 70% semantic (MedEmbed) + 30% lexical (BM25)
